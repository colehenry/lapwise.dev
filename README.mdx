# lapwise.dev

I built Lapwise because I wanted one place to follow Formula 1 without bouncing around different sites

It is made for people who care about what actually happened in a race weekend and not just the final order

The site is live at lapwise.dev

Right now you can check full race weekends, look through driver pages, compare team results, and explore circuit history

You can open Results, Drivers, Constructors, and Circuits and get the main parts of the project right away.

Next I want to make the driver, team, and circuit pages deeper, add a full season calendar, add head to head comparisons, add records pages, improve live race coverage, and post short race recaps between weekends.
